Workshop process

Clear the foam

  1. Release

    Begin by flushing the softened foam and dirt from the upper surfaces.

  2. Rinse

    Work down the panels, rinsing recesses around handles, trim and mirrors.

  3. Inspect

    Check the lower edges for trapped suds and remaining grit before moving on.

Service notes

Who is it for?

The stage immediately following a foam pre-wash.

What is covered?

A top-to-bottom body rinse and a visual check around exterior trim.

Scope and preparation

This stage clears loosened material. A hand wash and drying can be specified separately. Tell the technician about loose mouldings, leaking seals or damaged trim.

Why rinse from the roof down?

Runoff moves onto areas still waiting to be rinsed, which reduces repeated work on the lower panels.
