Workshop process

Loosen the road film

  1. Coat

    Cover the body evenly, working around mirrors, arches and lower panels.

  2. Dwell

    Let the foam soften the film. Adjust dwell to the product and conditions and rinse before it dries.

  3. Check

    Look for remaining heavy deposits and address those areas before contact washing.

Service notes

Who is it for?

Road film, dusty paint and regular exterior maintenance.

What is covered?

Foam application over the exterior, monitored dwell time and a check of the lower panels.

Scope and preparation

Foam needs a thorough rinse. Bonded contamination may still need a contact wash or separate treatment. Describe any existing wax, sealant or coating so the wash chemistry can be chosen accordingly.

Will the foam remove every spot?

Some tar, mineral deposits and embedded particles need a separate treatment after washing.
