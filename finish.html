Service notes

Who is it for?

The close of a detailing visit, particularly after polishing.

What is covered?

Visual review, removal of handling marks and a handover covering the selected work.

Scope and preparation

Protective wax, sealant and ceramic coating applications need a separate specification. Keep your estimate handy so each selected area can be checked at handover.

How should I maintain the finish?

Use clean wash media and follow the aftercare directions for any specific product applied during the visit.
