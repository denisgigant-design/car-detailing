Workshop process

Before the wash

  1. Survey

    Walk around the dry vehicle under a broad light to see road film, tar spots and visible marks.

  2. Inspect

    Look closely at lower doors, bumpers and panel edges where heavier dirt collects.

  3. Plan

    Record existing marks and agree the areas that need attention before washing.

Service notes

Who is it for?

A first visit, a car returning from winter driving, or paintwork with visible marks.

What is covered?

Panel and trim check, identification of heavy soiling, and a discussion of priorities.

Scope and preparation

Existing chips and scratches can be recorded here. Their repair is a separate job. Mention recent paint repairs, coated surfaces and any area you want left alone.

Can the inspection identify every mark?

Heavy dirt can hide fine defects. The technician can reassess these once the surface is clean.
